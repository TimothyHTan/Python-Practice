Where your work is saved and how the AI helper runs. These only change this computer.

Save folder

Your code, pseudocode, solved problems, attempts and mistakes are saved as normal files in their own folder, separate from the site. Clearing the browser never loses them: the site restores your work from this folder. When you switch to an empty folder, everything saved so far is copied there.
